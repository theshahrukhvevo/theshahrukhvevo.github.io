Arrangeo Privacy Policy

Effective 2 November 2026

Arrangeo is a puzzle game for Android. This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and the choices you have. Arrangeo has no accounts, logins or chat, and we never ask for your name, email, contacts, photos or location.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress and settings, such as levels completed, coins, energy, rewards and sound preferences, are saved only on your device. We do not receive this saved game. Uninstalling the game or using "Reset game progress" in Settings deletes it.

Advertising

Arrangeo is free and is supported by optional rewarded videos. A video only plays when you tap a WATCH button, and the game shows no other ads. The videos are provided by Google AdMob.

To show, measure and protect ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent the first time it opens, and personalized ads are only shown if you agree. You can change your answer at any time in Settings > Ad privacy choices. Anywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Learn how Google uses this information at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Game analytics and crash reports

We use Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to understand how the game is played and to fix problems. They receive events such as levels started and completed, rewards earned and whether a video finished, along with the app version, device model, operating system and a random app instance ID. Crash reports include technical details about the error. Analytics is set up so that it does not collect your advertising ID or use this information for advertising.

Learn more at firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

Purchases

If you buy an item in the game, the payment is handled by Google Play under Google's terms. We never see your card or payment details.

How information is shared

Information is shared only with Google, as our ads and analytics provider, for the purposes above. We do not sell your personal information for money. Some US state laws treat personalized advertising as "sharing" of personal information. Where those laws apply, you can opt out through the game's privacy choices or by deleting your advertising ID in Android settings.

Children

Arrangeo is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has used the game and want their information removed, contact us and we will help.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, to object to or restrict its use, and to withdraw consent. Because we do not hold an account or profile for you, most requests are handled by the privacy choices in the game, your Android settings, or Google directly. You can also contact us and we will respond within the time the law requires.

Security

Information sent to Google for ads, analytics and crash reports travels over encrypted connections. Your saved game stays on your device and is never uploaded by us.

How long information is kept

Your saved game stays on your device until you reset it or uninstall the game. Analytics data is deleted automatically after no more than 14 months, and crash reports after 90 days. Google keeps advertising data according to its own policies, linked above. To ask about deleting information, contact us using the details below.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.

Contact
